Indian Land Measurement Units

India’s heritage of land measurement embraces dozens of unique units, each woven into regional history and daily life. The size or value of a land unit can vary widely - not just from one state to another, but sometimes even within districts. Our database is a collaborative work-in-progress: we may not yet cover every local unit for every state, but you can help. Drop us a line, your contribution will help enrich this public resource, which is open to all on GitHub. Every value in this visualisation is expressed in square feet for clarity and easy comparison.
