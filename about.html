Indian Land Measurement Units
← Back to visualisation

India’s heritage of land measurement embraces dozens of unique units, each woven into regional history and daily life. The size or value of a land unit can vary widely — not just from one state to another, but sometimes even within districts. Our database is a collaborative work-in-progress: we may not yet cover every local unit for every state, but you can help. Fill this Google form, and your contribution will help enrich this public resource, which is open to all on GitHub. Every value in this visualisation is expressed in square feet for clarity and easy comparison.

Some entries are marked unverified (shown as a dashed, lighter bar with an asterisk) — these are the best estimates we could find, but they haven't been confirmed against an official or primary source, and in a few cases (like Manipur's historical land units) the underlying figure is itself disputed across sources. See SOURCES.md for the full citation list, and CONTRIBUTING.md if you'd like to confirm or correct one.
